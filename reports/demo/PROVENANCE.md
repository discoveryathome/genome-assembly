# Demo evidence

The inline demonstration shows the executed 250,000-base synthetic quality control in reports/control-validated/control.json. The correct assembly and deliberately inverted assembly have the same length and N50. QUAST reports zero versus two structural errors. The inversion reverses positions 60,000–119,999 (zero-based half-open [60000,120000)); the visual divides the circle's linear representation into schematic blocks of 60k, 60k and 130k bases. Blocks are not gene annotations. This is a checker demonstration, not the window101 performance result and not an organism assembled in the real world.

The desktop render and button interaction were inspected in Chrome. The flipped state showed B pointing left and the result changing to two structural errors; the reference stayed unchanged. The temporary narrow-viewport preview timed out; its override was reset. No external website was published.
